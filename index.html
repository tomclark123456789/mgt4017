Let’s break reselling down

There are two main sections to reselling, BUYING and SELLING, let’s start with buying. To become a good and successful reseller, you need to be able to analyse products and evaluate rough profit margins. Before diving into buying your first products you must learn what products sell and what don’t. There are many ways to gain this knowledge, all of which will be provided. When buying products, quality control is key, you should only be dealing with functional and high quality goods to minimise returns. That was a quick overview on the buying half, now onto selling. To gain maximum possible revenue, you need to ensure many things:

  • good quality products
  • good profit margins
  • good listing photos
  • clear and helpful description / title

They are just some of the things you need to think about when selling your products, more will be provided.

Those are the basics of reselling and they are fundamental to allow you to be a profitable reseller. However just following those vague principles will not take you as far as you want to go. Below we will dive far deeper into what it really takes to get consistent sales and high profits.
